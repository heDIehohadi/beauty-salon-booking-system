# Problem Definition

## 1. Project Overview

The project is a beauty salon appointment booking system.

Its purpose is to improve appointment management for customers,
hairstylists, and salon managers.

## 2. Problem Statement

Traditional appointment booking in beauty salons may rely on
manual communication and record-keeping.

This can lead to human errors, overlapping appointments,
inefficient appointment management, and insufficient consideration
of hairstylists' working schedules and availability.

The proposed system aims to centralize appointment management,
make availability visible, and prevent conflicting bookings.

## 3. Project Goals

- Simplify appointment booking.
- Allow customers to view available appointment slots.
- Reduce appointment scheduling errors.
- Prevent conflicting bookings.
- Improve appointment management for the salon.

## 4. Initial Scope

### In Scope

- Viewing salon services and prices.
- Viewing available hairstylists.
- Viewing available appointment slots.
- Booking appointments.
- Viewing personal appointments.
- Cancelling appointments.
- Rescheduling appointments.

### Out of Scope

- Customer reviews and ratings.
- Loyalty points and reward programs.
- Discount programs.
- Online payments.
- SMS notifications.

## 5. Assumptions and Open Questions

- The exact responsibilities of hairstylists and salon managers
  have not been finalized.
- Appointment cancellation and rescheduling rules
  have not been defined.
- The salon's working hours and hairstylists' availability
  need further analysis.
- Appointment duration rules need further analysis.

- The salon manager is responsible for managing hairstylists'
  working hours, breaks, and leave periods.
- Hairstylists cannot directly modify their availability
  in the initial version.
